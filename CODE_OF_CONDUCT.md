# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

HanLearn is intended to be a welcoming, inclusive, and constructive open-source community. We are committed to providing a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, language, nationality, race, religion, sexual orientation, socioeconomic status, technical background, or level of experience.

This policy follows the community standards of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing the impact of words and actions.
- Welcome different viewpoints and beginner questions.
- Give feedback about ideas and code, not personal characteristics.
- Use inclusive language and respect names, pronouns, cultures, and language communities.
- Protect privacy and avoid sharing personal, educational, or security-sensitive information.
- Follow the project's license, security policy, and maintainer instructions.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help repair harm when possible.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, stalking, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted advances.
- Deliberate misgendering, deadnaming, or hateful conduct.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Credential theft, malicious code, doxxing, or other harmful activity.
- Spam, impersonation, plagiarism, or knowingly submitting misleading content.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional and collaborative environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, chat channels, events, and any other space where a person represents HanLearn or interacts with the project community. It also applies when conduct outside project spaces has a clear and harmful effect on the safety or participation of the community.

Project maintainers may clarify or apply this policy based on the context of an incident.

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

1. Preserve relevant context, such as links, dates, screenshots, or messages.
2. Do not start a public argument or publish sensitive information.
3. Contact the repository owner or maintainers privately through GitHub.
4. For a security vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Reports should include what happened, where it happened, who was involved, and any supporting evidence that can be shared safely. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports fairly and may ask for additional information. They will take action proportionate to the behavior and its impact, which may include:

- A private or public request to stop the behavior.
- A warning or moderation of content.
- Temporary or permanent restriction from project spaces.
- Removal of contributions or access.
- Referral to the appropriate authorities when required by law or necessary for safety.

Retaliation is itself a violation. A maintainer with a conflict of interest should not participate in the decision.

## Acknowledgment

By participating in HanLearn spaces, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and the project's contribution and security policies. Thank you for helping make the project safe and useful for the community.
